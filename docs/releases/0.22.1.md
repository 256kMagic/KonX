# KonX 0.22.1 — AI context and draft protection

2026-10-23. Prepare AI context without cutting through secrets and protect unfinished AI input.

## Changes

- Redact complete bounded context before shortening the preview. Oversized fields are omitted whole, and coverage notices explain omitted fields, records, and shortened excerpts.
- Preserve unsent questions, edited context, and knowledge-note drafts through cancellation or failure. Closing an AI tab asks before discarding drafts; updates wait for unfinished input.
- Retain encrypted provider credentials, explicit sharing, and offline knowledge search.

Read [AI & Knowledge](../AI_KNOWLEDGE_GUIDE.md), the [user guide](../USER_GUIDE.md), and [Updating KonX](../UPDATING.md).

## Downloads and upgrading

Windows x64: [installer](https://github.com/256kMagic/KonX/releases/download/v0.22.1/KonX-Setup-0.22.1.exe), [portable](https://github.com/256kMagic/KonX/releases/download/v0.22.1/KonX-0.22.1-portable.exe), and [checksums](https://github.com/256kMagic/KonX/releases/download/v0.22.1/SHA256SUMS.txt).

Installed 0.6.2+ and portable 0.6.0+ can use **Update KonX**. Installed 0.6.0/0.6.1 requires one manual upgrade. Save drafts, stop listeners, and disconnect before restarting; the existing profile remains in use.
