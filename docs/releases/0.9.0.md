# KonX 0.9.0 — RTP media and audio

Packet Capture and the SIP ladder now open a media view for directional RTP streams, packet timing, G.711 audio, and WAV exports.

## Features

- SDP-associated stream and call filtering, endpoints, SSRC, codec mappings, sequence gaps, duplicates, reordering, and jitter.
- Clickable source packets and jitter plots, RTCP sender/receiver reports, and explicit incomplete or ambiguous coverage.
- Waveform, explicit G.711 playback, capture-time aligned paired playback, and PCM16 WAV export per direction.
- A bundled synthetic SIP/RTP tone demo with deliberate missing observations and reordering.
- Audio cleanup when dialogs close or captures are replaced. Heuristic RTP identification and audio interpretation are opt-in.

See the [RTP media guide](../RTP_MEDIA.md), [user guide](../USER_GUIDE.md), and [phased roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).

## Upgrade

Use **Update KonX** from installed 0.6.2 or later or portable 0.6.0 or later. Older installed copies need a manual upgrade; see [Updating KonX](../UPDATING.md).

## Coverage

Audio reconstruction supports unencrypted mono PCMU/PCMA at 8 kHz. SRTP, encrypted signaling, and unsupported codecs remain unavailable. Sequence gaps and missing directions describe capture observations, not proven network loss or receiver experience. Paired playback uses capture time, not synchronized sender clocks.

The six inherited AI, Wi-Fi, and security-assessment findings listed in [0.6.3](0.6.3.md#known-issues) remain unchanged.
